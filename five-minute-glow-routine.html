A 5-Minute Glow Routine for Busy Mornings

Published October 9, 2026

Save to Pinterest

You do not need ten steps to look fresh in the morning. This quick routine takes about five minutes and focuses on skin that feels comfortable and looks naturally bright.

Step 1: Cleanse gently

Use lukewarm water and a mild cleanser. In the morning your skin does not need a heavy deep clean, so a gentle wash is enough to remove overnight oil and sweat.

Step 2: Add a lightweight serum

A light serum goes on while the skin is still slightly damp. Many people like hydrating formulas for a dewy finish. Press it into your skin with your fingertips instead of rubbing.

Step 3: Moisturize

Even oily skin needs moisture. Choose a light gel or lotion in the morning and a richer cream at night if your skin feels dry.

Step 4: Never skip sunscreen

Sunscreen is the step that protects everything else you do. Apply it as the last skincare step, and use enough to cover your face and neck. Reapply if you are outdoors for long periods.

Step 5: Add colour in one minute

  • A soft lip tint in a rosy shade
  • A cream blush tapped on the cheeks with your fingers
  • Brow gel to brush your brows into shape
  • Optional: a neutral eyeshadow wash if you have an extra minute

A few reminders

Patch test any new product on a small area first, and introduce only one new product at a time. If you have a skin condition or react badly to products, speak to a dermatologist. This article shares routine ideas and is not medical advice.
